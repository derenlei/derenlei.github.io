### [Microsoft](https://careers.microsoft.com/students/internships)
**Development (engineering)**    
- **Program manager**    
  1. A bachelor's, master's, or doctorate degree in engineering, computer science or related field (pursuing with at least one semester/quarter remaining after internship).  
  2. A year or two of experience programming in C++, Java or other computer programming languages preferred.    
  3. A year or two of experience in C/C++/C#/VB.NET and experience designing and developing tool architecture preferred.
  4. Familiarity with managing complex project schedules, solving complex problems and nurturing cross-group collaboration.
  5. Strong technical prowess, including understanding of algorithms, systems architecture and end-user experience.
  6. Demonstrated expertise in feature definition, design and feasibility.
  7. Demonstrated skill in estimating development time.
  8. Demonstrated skills in negotiation and conflict management.     
- **Software engineer**
  1. A bachelor's, master's or doctorate degree in engineering, computer science or related field (pursuing with at least one semester/quarter remaining after internship).
  2. A year or two of experience programming in C++, Java or other computer programming languages preferred.
  3. A year or two of experience in C/C++/C#/VB.NET and experience designing and developing tool architecture preferred.
  4. Strong technical prowess, including understanding of algorithms, systems architecture and end-user experience.
  5. Demonstrated expertise in feature definition, design and feasibility.
  6. Demonstrated skill in estimating development time.
  7. Demonstrated skills in negotiation and conflict management.     

**Data & applied sciences** (engineering)
- **Data scientist**
  1. Engineering experience using large data systems on SQL, Hadoop, etc.
  2. Proficiency using one or more programming or scripting language to work with data such as: Python, Perl, or C#.
  3. Experience performing data analysis and applying statistics working with tools such as: Excel, R, MATLAB, AMPL, or SAS.
  4. Experience with product and service telemetry systems.
  5. Passion to learn how to be an excellent Data scientist from your peers, manager, and other stakeholders.
  6. Ability to interact with peers and stakeholders to drive product and business impact.
  7. Strong interpersonal and communications skills.
